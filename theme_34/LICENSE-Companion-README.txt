Companion — Veterinary & Pet Care Website Template
==================================================

Companion is a warm, modern, two-page website template for veterinary clinics, animal
hospitals and pet-care practices. It includes a homepage and a detailed Services & Booking
page, with an appointment mini-form, animated trust stats, a services grid, meet-the-vets
cards, a booking timeline, pet-care blog teasers, a testimonials slider, an FAQ accordion,
wellness-plan pricing and a 24/7 emergency line. Built on self-hosted Bootstrap 5 with
100% vanilla JavaScript (no jQuery), it is fast, responsive, accessible and progressively
enhanced so content stays visible without scripts. Companion is free for personal and
commercial use — just add your own images, connect the booking forms to your handler, and
tweak the colors via the CSS custom properties in css/style.css.
