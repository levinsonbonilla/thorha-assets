Serene — Free Spa & Wellness Website Template by uiCookies

Concept: "Candlelit Hammam." Serene deliberately avoids the usual spa cliche of sage
green + warm cream + terracotta. Instead the calm comes from a cool blue-slate ink over a
warm greige background, softened by a pale blue-grey mist and lit by a single gilded amber
accent that stands in for candlelight. The signature move is a tall arch shape — a hammam
doorway — used to frame the hero, about, gallery and team images, paired with a dotted-leader
"spa menu" for the treatment list. Corners are used with intent: arches for imagery, small
radii for cards, full pills for buttons.

Palette:
  #22303B  ink (deep blue-slate — text, dark sections, footer)
  #58707A  slate (muted teal-slate — subheads, borders)
  #DDE4E3  mist (pale blue-grey — soft section tints, cards)
  #ECE6DD  sand (warm greige — page background)
  #BC864A  amber (gilded accent — CTAs, prices, candlelight)
  #CDA9A0  blush (dusty clay-rose — used sparingly)

Fonts (Google Fonts):
  Display — Fraunces (soft optical serif, weights 300–500, italics for emphasis)
  Body/UI — Jost (airy geometric humanist sans, weights 300–600)

Built with Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted) and vanilla JavaScript — no jQuery, no CDN scripts, no
icon fonts (all icons are inline SVG). Fully responsive 360px–1440px, accessible landmarks and
focus styles, and prefers-reduced-motion aware.

Free for personal and commercial use.
