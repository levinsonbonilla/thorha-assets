Pulse — Free Clinic & Hospital Website Template (uiCookies)

Concept: A calm, modern multi-specialty hospital site that treats online appointment
booking as the hero, not a buried contact form. The recurring visual idea is an ECG
heartbeat line — it appears in the logo mark, the hero "live wait time" badge, and as
a faint pulse running behind the dark stats band — tying the brand name to the design.
The layout is deliberately airy and asymmetric (floating cards over image frames)
rather than the boxy dark-navbar look common to free medical themes.

Palette: cobalt-indigo #274FD4 (primary, with #1C3AAE for hover) as the trustworthy
clinical anchor; a coral "pulse" accent #EC4B5A for heartbeat lines, ratings and small
highlights; a hued cool-white paper #F4F6FC page background with #DDE4F1 hairlines
(neither pure grey nor cream); deep slate navy #142033 for headings and dark bands;
and a soft sage #E9F4EE / #2C8A5B for the positive "insurance accepted" zone. This
avoids both generic medical teal and the navy+orange of the sibling Health template.

Font pairing: Lexend for display/headings (a typeface engineered for reading
proficiency — an intentional, on-brand choice for a healthcare site that values clarity)
paired with Mulish for body copy (a warm, humanist sans that keeps long text friendly
and readable). Neither is Inter or Space Grotesk.

Tech: self-hosted Bootstrap 5 + one vanilla JS file (no jQuery), inline-SVG icons only
(no icon fonts), Google Fonts via link. Deploys clean on a strict-CSP host.
