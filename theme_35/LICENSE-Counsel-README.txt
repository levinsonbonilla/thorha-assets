Counsel — Free Bootstrap 5 Law Firm Website Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Counsel is a free, authoritative website template for law firms, attorneys, and
legal practices, built on Bootstrap 5. It ships two pages — a full home page and a
detailed practice-areas page — with a deep-navy-and-gold identity, Fraunces serif
headings, and Inter body text that reads senior and trustworthy. It is completely
jQuery-free: the sticky navbar, count-up stats, testimonial slider, native FAQ
accordion, and the validated hero case-evaluation form are all plain vanilla
JavaScript, with scroll animations built on IntersectionObserver, progressively
enhanced (all content is visible without JS), and respectful of prefers-reduced-motion.
Free for personal and commercial use — see https://uicookies.com/license/.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html            Home page (all sections)
  practice-areas.html   Detailed practice-area descriptions
  css/vendor/           Bootstrap 5 (minified, self-hosted)
  css/style.css         Template design layer (palette, type, components)
  js/vendor/            Bootstrap 5 bundle (includes Popper)
  js/main.js            Template behavior — vanilla JavaScript, no jQuery
  img/                  Demo images

NOTES
  - Built on Bootstrap 5. No jQuery, no plugin dependencies.
  - Forms are front-end only; point the form action at your backend or a
    form service to make them live.
  - Replace the demo images in img/ with your own optimized photos before launch.
