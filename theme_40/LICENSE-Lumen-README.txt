Lumen — Free Skincare & Cosmetics Website Template by uiCookies

Concept: "Dewglow Lab." Lumen is a product-led clean-beauty brand — a skincare store, not
a treatment salon — so it is deliberately distinct from the services-based spa sibling in this
category. The identity leans clinical-but-warm: a deep botanical pine over warm milk paper,
lit by a signature apricot "glow" accent (evoking a Vitamin C serum) and cooled by a glacial
aqua that stands in for hydration. The recurring device is the ingredient "spec chip" — the
exact percentage of every active printed like a lab spec (12% Vitamin C, 2% Hyaluronic) —
reinforced by dewdrop bullets, glow-backed product bottles, a live add-to-bag counter and a
draggable before/after results slider. Corners are soft rounded rectangles, not pills; imagery
sits in tall product frames with a subtle glow-to-aqua gradient behind each bottle.

Palette:
  #1f322a  ink    — deep botanical green-charcoal (text, dark bands, footer)
  #2f5d48  pine   — rich pine (primary brand, buttons, links)
  #e29a6b  glow   — warm apricot glow (signature accent, prices, highlights)
  #cfe4dd  aqua   — glacial aqua (hydration tints, science section)
  #f2f1e9  milk   — warm milk paper (page background, cool green undertone)
  #fbfbf6  shell  — near-white (raised cards)

Fonts (Google Fonts):
  Display — Bricolage Grotesque (contemporary editorial grotesque, optical sizing, 400–800)
  Body/UI — Hanken Grotesk (clean humanist sans, 300–800)

Built with Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted) and vanilla JavaScript — no jQuery, no CDN scripts, no
icon fonts (every icon is inline SVG). Fully responsive 360px–1440px, with semantic landmarks,
a skip link, visible focus styles, alt text, aria labels and prefers-reduced-motion support.
